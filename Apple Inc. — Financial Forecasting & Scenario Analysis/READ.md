# Apple Inc. — Financial Forecasting & Scenario Analysis

## Objective

Built a financial forecasting model using Apple's publicly reported financial
data from SEC Form 10-K filings.

The objective was to analyze historical financial performance and develop a
two-year forecast under multiple business scenarios.

## Company

Apple Inc.

## Data Source

SEC Form 10-K filings.

Historical period:
- FY2021
- FY2022
- FY2023
- FY2024
- FY2025

Forecast period:
- FY2026E
- FY2027E

All historical financial figures are reported in USD millions.

## Analysis

The model covers:

- Revenue growth
- Gross profit and gross margin
- Operating expenses
- Operating income
- EBITDA
- Net income
- Operating cash flow
- Capital expenditure
- Free cash flow
- Scenario analysis

## Scenario Analysis

Three scenarios were modeled:

- Downside
- Base
- Upside

Revenue growth assumptions are adjusted across scenarios to evaluate the
potential impact on revenue, EBITDA and net income.
