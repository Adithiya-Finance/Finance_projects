

This project analyzes **Accounts Payable (AP)** and working-capital-related metrics using transaction-level invoice data.

The project demonstrates how finance teams can analyze:

- Outstanding Accounts Payable

- Overdue invoices

- Invoice aging

- Vendor concentration

- Payment timing

- Department-level payment performance

- Early-payment discount opportunities

The dataset is a **synthetic portfolio simulation** created for learning and demonstration purposes.

---

## Business Objective

The analysis answers the following questions:

1. How much Accounts Payable is currently outstanding?

2. How much AP is overdue?

3. Which vendors have the largest outstanding balances?

4. Which invoices are significantly overdue?

5. Which departments have payment bottlenecks?

6. What is the average payment time?

7. What early-payment discount opportunities exist?

8. What are the working-capital implications?
