# Canara Bank vs Axis Bank — Financial Statement Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the five-year financial performance of **Canara Bank and Axis Bank** using reported financial data.

The analysis focuses on banking-specific financial metrics such as:

- Net Interest Income (NII)
- Net Profit
- Total Assets
- Total Deposits
- Advances
- Gross NPA
- Net NPA
- Return on Assets (ROA)
- Return on Equity (ROE)
- Net Interest Margin (NIM)

The objective is to understand how the two banks performed across growth, profitability, asset quality and banking-sector KPIs.

---

## Business Objective

The analysis answers the following questions:

1. How did Net Interest Income change over five years?
2. How did profitability evolve?
3. How rapidly did deposits and advances grow?
4. How did asset quality change?
5. How did ROA and ROE change?
6. What are the major financial trends visible across the five-year period?

## Data Sources

Primary sources include:

- Canara Bank Annual Reports
- Axis Bank Annual Reports
- Axis Bank Statutory Reports

The monetary values are standardized to **₹ crore** and ratios are represented as percentages.
